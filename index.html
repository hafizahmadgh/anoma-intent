About Anoma Intent Forge

What is Intent-Based Trading?

Intent-based trading shifts from specifying how to execute to declaring what you want. Anoma's protocol then locates the optimal route(s) and counterparties to fulfill the user's intent, often spanning multiple chains and markets. That means better prices, less wasted gas, and lower MEV exposure compared to traditional single-path transactions.

Why Anoma is Faster & Better vs Traditional Flows

  • Parallel matching: multiple potential matches are evaluated in parallel, reducing the time-to-execution.
  • Optimized routing: the matcher can combine swaps, aggregators and cross-chain hops into a single atomic settlement.
  • Reduced retries: fewer failed transactions by matching intent constraints off-chain before execution.
  • Privacy layer: optional privacy preserves user strategy during matching, preventing front-running.
